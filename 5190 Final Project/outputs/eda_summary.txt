Cleaning procedure:
- Source labels were extracted from the article URLs.
- URLs were not used as model features because the task is to classify source from headline text only.
- Headlines were lightly cleaned by normalizing whitespace.
- Rows with missing or invalid headlines were removed.
- Rows with unknown source domains were removed.
- Exact duplicate headlines were removed.
- Numeric labels were created with FoxNews = 1 and NBC = 0.
- The dataset was split into train, validation, and test sets using stratified sampling.
